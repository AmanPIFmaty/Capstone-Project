Allow the app to access your device features
============================================
Allow the app to access your device features
Do one of the following depending on where in Teams you’re using the app:
When you're using the app in Teams: Select
More options
and choose
App permissions
.
Use the toggle(s) to turn on device access.
If Teams asks for access to a device, select
App permissions
. In the dialog, toggle the relevant device feature settings on.
Close the dialog and refresh the app to apply the changes.


Allow the browser to access your device features
================================================
Allow the browser to access your device features
Give your browser permission to access your camera or other relevant device features the app wants to use.
Check your browser settings:
You may need to “allow” your camera and/or microphone to work in Teams.
Contact your IT admin:
Some settings may be managed by your IT admin. Contact them for more details.
Note:
Microsoft Teams on the web is currently supported in Chrome, Safari, Edge, and Firefox.
Google Chrome
Open
Settings
.
Select
Privacy and security
>
Site settings
>
Permissions
.
Select
Camera
.
At the top of this screen, make sure your correct device is selected from the dropdown menu.
Under
Default behavior
, select
Sites can ask to use your camera
.
Safari
In Safari, open
Preferences
.
Select
Websites
.
In
General
, select
Camera
.
Next to
When visiting other websites
, select
Ask
or
Allow
.
Microsoft Edge
Select
Settings and more
>
Settings
.
Select
Cookies and site permissions
.
Select Camera.
Turn on the
Ask before accessing (recommended)
toggle.
Note:
If this is turned off, access to your camera will be blocked on Edge.
​​​​​​​Mozilla Firefox
In Firefox, open
Settings
.
Select
Privacy & Security
.
Under
Permissions
, find
Camera
or
Microphone
, then select
Settings
.
Make sure access is allowed for the Teams site.
Close Settings, then refresh the Teams app in your browser.
